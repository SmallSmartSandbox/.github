# SmallSmartSandbox

SmallSmartSandbox is an experimental project focused on testing and studying autonomous AI behavior in controlled environments.

Our goal is to explore AI capabilities, observe emerging behaviors, and develop methods for monitoring, evaluating, and improving AI safety.

## Focus Areas

- Autonomous AI behavior
- AI safety and ethics
- Model evaluation
- Monitoring and containment
- Local AI experimentation

## Status

Early development. The project is experimental, and its methods and goals may evolve over time.
